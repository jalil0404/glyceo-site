The glycemic index

How quickly a food containing carbohydrates raises blood sugar, compared with pure glucose (100).

  • Low: up to 55
  • Medium: 56 to 69
  • High: 70 and over

The glycemic load

It takes into account how much you actually eat. A high-index food can have a small load if the portion is modest. A load counts as low up to 10 and high from 20. Glyami adds it up over the day, with a guide value of 80 that you can adjust.

Where the numbers come from

Glycemic indexes. Estimated from published tables (Atkinson et al., 2008 and 2021; University of Sydney database) and from the product's composition.
