Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This is a translation. In case of difference, the French version prevails.

Key points

  • The Application operates without an account, without advertising, without audience measurement and without trackers. The Site makes use of simple anonymous audience measurement, without cookies.
  • The data entered by the User (profile, readings, meals, history) are stored exclusively on the User's phone. The Publisher has no access to them.
  • The only Internet connections made by the Application are to Open Food Facts: looking up a product, its photographs and alternatives and, where the User so decides, sharing the entry for an unknown product. They contain no profile data and no health data.
  • The User may erase all of their data at any time from within the Application.

Article 1 – Purpose and scope

This privacy policy (the “Policy”) describes how personal data are processed in connection with the use of the Glyami mobile application (the “Application”) and of the website accessible at glyami.app (the “Site”). It is drawn up in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of 27 April 2016 (the “GDPR”) and with French Act No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978, as amended, on information technology, data files and civil liberties.

It applies to any person who installs or uses the Application, or who visits the Site (the “User”). Glyami was known as Glycéo until October 2026.

Article 2 – Data controller

The Application and the Site are published by Abdeljalil NEAMI, an independent developer (Neami Labs), in France (the “Publisher”).

For any question relating to the Policy or to the exercise of their rights, the User may write to contact@glyami.app.

The Publisher has not designated a data protection officer, such a designation not being mandatory in view of the processing operations which the Publisher carries out.

Article 3 – Principle: data stored on the User's device

The Application operates without an account and without registration. There is no Publisher server. The data which the User enters in the Application are stored solely in the memory of the User's phone. The Publisher does not receive them, does not see them and cannot consult them.

It follows that the Publisher does not collect, host or use any personal data by means of the Application, with the sole exception of the correspondence which the User chooses to send to the Publisher (Article 8).

Article 4 – Data stored on the device

Depending on the features which the User chooses to use, the User may store in the Application:

  • their profile: first name, date of birth, height, weight, language, text size and daily load guide (and, where the installed version offers blood glucose tracking, their situation and their unit of measurement);
  • health information which the User records personally: weight, waist size, meals, walking days and, where the installed version offers blood glucose tracking, blood glucose readings, HbA1c and personal tests;
  • their loyalty cards: the name which the User gives them, their code and, if the User so chooses, their photograph;
  • their shopping lists and their purchases;
  • their saved plates, their answers to the “Did you know?” cards and the products which the User copies from their labels;
  • the history of scanned products and the User's alert preferences (allergies, food choices).

The entry of each of these items of information is optional.

Some of this information constitutes data concerning health, a special category of data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR, to which the law attaches enhanced protection. It is processed exclusively on the User's device, under the User's sole control, and is not transmitted to the Publisher or to any third party, save where the User decides to share it under the conditions set out in Article 7.

Article 5 – Internet connections made by the Application

When the User scans a product or carries out a search, the Application queries Open Food Facts, an open and collaborative food database. The request contains only:

  • the barcode or the search term;
  • for the “smart swap” feature (less sugary alternatives): the Open Food Facts category of the product displayed and, where the phone is set to one of the supported countries, the code of that country, derived from the phone's region setting and not from the User's location;
  • the name and version of the Application, together with a contact address, as required by Open Food Facts.

No profile data and no readings are transmitted. As with any Internet connection, the IP address of the device is visible to the servers of Open Food Facts, which processes it on its own behalf, in accordance with its own privacy policy. Product photographs are loaded from its image servers.

Unknown product. Where a product is not listed in Open Food Facts, the User may copy its label into the Application: the entry remains on the User's phone. If the installed version offers this option and the User decides to share the entry, the Application transmits to Open Food Facts only the information relating to the product (barcode, name, brand, nutrition values, ingredients, language of the entry), to the exclusion of any information relating to the User. That information becomes public, under the ODbL licence. Where no network connection is available, the submission is held pending on the phone.

Products which have been viewed are kept in memory on the phone so that they can be viewed again without a connection. Meal ideas, shopping lists, loyalty cards and all calculations work without a connection.

Article 6 – Permissions requested on the device

  • Camera: to read barcodes and, if the User so chooses, to photograph their loyalty card. Codes are read live; the photograph of a card remains on the phone. Nothing is transmitted.
  • Notifications: only where the installed version offers reminders and the User turns them on. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, without any server.

The User may revoke these permissions at any time in the settings of their phone.

Article 7 – Sharing carried out at the User's initiative

The User may create a report in PDF format, a share card, a backup file or an invitation message, or send a shopping list by message or by means of a code to scan. These items are created on the phone and leave it only if the User personally chooses a destination application (messaging, email, storage…) or personally presents the code. The items so transmitted are then subject to the rules of the chosen application and of their recipient, over which the Publisher exercises no control.

Article 8 – Correspondence with the Publisher

Where the User writes to the Publisher from the “An idea, an issue?” section, the message is sent from the User's own email application. The Publisher then receives the User's email address, the content of the message and a technical line (version of the Application, phone model, system version, language).

These data are processed in order to reply to the User and to improve the Application, on the basis of the Publisher's legitimate interest in following up the requests addressed to the Publisher (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). They are retained for the period necessary to deal with the request and to follow it up. They are neither transferred nor disclosed to third parties, with the exception of the technical service provider which hosts the Publisher's email service.

Article 9 – Backup and change of phone

The Application asks the Android system not to include its data in the automatic backup of the User's Google account. In order to change phone, the User uses the Application's own backup (Settings → My data): the User obtains a file which they keep and restore themselves. The photographs of loyalty cards are not included in it. As this file is not encrypted, it is the User's responsibility to keep it in a safe place.

Article 10 – What the Publisher undertakes not to do

  • No account and no registration.
  • In the Application: no advertising, no audience measurement, no trackers.
  • No third-party data collection or analytics tools in the Application.
  • No sale, no rental and no sharing of data by the Publisher.

The Publisher does not carry out any profiling or any automated decision-making concerning the User.

Article 11 – Retention period and deletion

The data stored in the Application remain on the phone for as long as the User keeps them there. The User may at any time modify them, export them or erase them in their entirety from Settings → Erase all my data. Uninstalling the Application also results in the deletion of its data, with the exception of the files which the User has exported.

Article 12 – Security

As the data are stored only on the User's phone, their security depends first and foremost on the security of that device. The User is advised to protect their phone with a passcode or another locking mechanism, and to keep in a safe place the files which they export.

Article 13 – Recipients and transfers outside the European Union

The Publisher does not disclose the data stored in the Application to any recipient: they leave the phone only where the User so decides, in the cases described in Articles 5 and 7. The only third parties which may be involved are:

  • Open Food Facts, for the requests described in Article 5;
  • the host of the Site and the audience measurement tool, solely for the data described in Article 16;
  • the Publisher's email service provider, for the correspondence referred to in Article 8.

The host of the Site, GitHub, Inc., is established in the United States of America: the technical logs which it keeps may be processed there. GitHub, Inc. states, in its privacy statement, that it complies with the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and relies on the standard contractual clauses published by the European Commission. The Application is distributed by Google Play, whose terms and privacy policy apply to the download of the Application.

Article 14 – Rights of the User

The GDPR and French Act No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 confer on the User, under the conditions laid down therein, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability in respect of the data concerning the User, the right to object, and the right to give instructions concerning the fate of their data after their death.

Apart from the emails which the User sends to the Publisher, the Publisher holds no data concerning the User: these rights are therefore exercised directly in the Application, which allows the data to be viewed, modified, exported and erased. In respect of their correspondence, or for any question, the User may write to contact@glyami.app.

The User may also lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07 (cnil.fr), or with the supervisory authority of their place of residence.

Article 15 – Minors

The Application is intended for adults. A minor must use it with a responsible adult and with their doctor.

Article 16 – The Site

The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It uses no cookies and carries no advertising. The host may keep technical logs, including the IP address, for the security of the service, in accordance with its privacy statement.

Audience measurement. In order to know the number of visitors to the Site and where they come from, the Publisher uses GoatCounter, an open-source tool which operates without cookies. This tool records the page viewed, the referring site, the language, the type of browser and of device, and the country derived from the IP address. The IP address is not retained and no identifier makes it possible to follow a visitor from one visit to the next: the Publisher has access only to totals. Where the browser sends the “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signal, no visit is counted. This measurement is based on the Publisher's legitimate interest in knowing the volume of visits to the Site (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

This choice is stored in the User's browser, without any cookie.

The former address glyceo.app redirects to the Site.

Article 17 – Amendment of the Policy

The Publisher may amend the Policy, in particular in order to take account of changes to the Application or to the applicable regulations. The version in force is the one published on this page, the date of the last update of which appears at the top. Any significant amendment will also be notified in the Application.

Article 18 – Governing law

The Policy is governed by French law, without prejudice to the mandatory provisions applicable in the User's country of residence.
