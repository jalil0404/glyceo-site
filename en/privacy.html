Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This is a translation. In case of difference, the French version prevails.

In short

  • In the app: no account, no advertising, no analytics, no trackers. On this website: a simple anonymous visit count, with no cookies.
  • Everything you enter (profile, readings, meals, history) stays on your phone. We have no access to it.
  • The only Internet connection is used to look up a product in Open Food Facts. It contains no personal or health data.
  • You can erase everything at any time from the app.

Who publishes Glycéo?

Glycéo is a free app published by Abdeljalil NEAMI, independent developer (Neami Labs), in France. For any question: contact@glyceo.app.

The data you enter in the app

Depending on what you choose to fill in, the app may store:

  • your profile: first name, date of birth, height, weight, waist measurement and language;
  • information you record yourself: weigh-ins and meals;
  • the history of scanned products, your shopping list and your alert preferences (allergies, food choices).

Everything is optional. This data is stored only in your phone's memory. There is no Glycéo server: we do not receive it, see it or have any way to access it.

Some of this information is health data, which the law gives special protection. It is processed only on your device, under your sole control.

What goes over the Internet

When you scan a product or search, the app queries Open Food Facts, an open and collaborative food database. The request contains only:

  • the barcode or the search term;
  • for the “smart swap” (less sugary alternatives): the Open Food Facts category of the product shown and, if your phone is set to one of the supported countries, that country's code (taken from your phone's region setting, not your location);
  • the app name and version ("Glyceo/version (contact@glyceo.app)"), required by Open Food Facts.

No profile data or readings are ever sent. As with any connection, your IP address is visible to Open Food Facts servers, which use it according to their own privacy policy. Product photos are loaded from their image servers.

Unknown product. If a product isn't in Open Food Facts, you can copy its label into the app: the entry stays on your phone. If you choose to share it, the app sends Open Food Facts, using Glycéo's contributor account, only the product information (barcode, name, brand, nutrition values, ingredients). Nothing about you. This information becomes public under the ODbL licence. With no connection, it waits on your phone.

Products you look up are kept on your phone so you can see them again offline.

Permissions requested

  • Camera: only to read barcodes. The image is analysed live on your phone; it is neither saved nor sent.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

When you share something

You can create a PDF report, a share card, a backup file or an invitation message. They are created on your phone and only leave it if you choose a destination app yourself (messaging, email, storage…). What you send is then subject to the rules of that app and of your recipient.

Phone backup

If Android automatic backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. This backup is managed by Google according to your settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no trackers or cookies.
  • No third-party data collection or analytics tools in the app.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone for as long as you keep it. You can edit it, export it or erase everything from Settings → Erase all my data. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data, except files you have exported and any Android backup.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you in particular the rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. As we hold no data about you, you exercise these rights directly in the app (view, edit, export, erase). For any question, write to contact@glyceo.app. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Who the app is for

Glycéo is intended for adults. A minor should use it with a responsible adult and with their doctor.

This website

The glyceo.app website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It uses no cookies and no advertising. The host may keep technical logs (including IP addresses) for security, according to its privacy statement.

Visit statistics. To know how many people visit the site and where they come from, we use GoatCounter, an open-source tool with no cookies. It counts the page viewed, the site you came from, the language, the browser and device type, and the country derived from your IP address. Your IP address is not stored and no identifier follows you from one visit to the next: we only see totals. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, nothing is counted.

This choice is kept in your browser, with no cookie.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. Any significant change will also be announced in the app.
