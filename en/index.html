Everything Glycéo does

Scan a product, look up a food, build your plate, do your shopping: Glycéo is with you all the way.

Scan

The barcode is enough: Pépin finds the product and what's in it. Product not found? Copy the label and it gets calculated anyway.

Understand

Estimated glycemic index and glycemic load for the portion you actually eat, with a simple colour: gentle, moderate or worth watching.

Your daily load

Your meals add up in a gauge with a guide value of 80. You see where you stand at a glance, no maths needed.

My shopping

A tile-based list sorted by aisle: over 500 illustrated items with their estimated GI, seasonal fruit and veg, and one-tap adding.

My plate

Build your meal, say pasta, vegetables and chicken: Glycéo estimates the load of the whole meal, not just one product.

Hidden sugars, allergies, pork-free, alcohol-free: Pépin checks the ingredients at every scan and lets you know.

Frequently asked questions

What is the glycemic index?

The glycemic index (GI) shows how quickly a food containing carbohydrates raises blood sugar compared with pure glucose (100). GI is low up to 55, moderate from 56 to 69 and high from 70. Glycéo shows an estimated GI based on published scientific tables.

And the glycemic load?

The glycemic load (GL) takes into account how much you actually eat: GL = GI × carbohydrates in the portion (grams) ÷ 100. A high-GI food can have a small load if the portion is modest. Glycéo calculates it for your portion and adds up your meals in your daily load.

Is Glycéo really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere: everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no Glycéo server. The only connection is used to look up a product in an open food database, with no personal data.

Does Glycéo replace my doctor?

No. Glycéo gives nutrition information and estimates. It is not a medical device: the app does not diagnose, does not calculate doses and does not replace the advice of your doctor or dietitian.

Who is Glycéo for?

For any adult who wants to understand how meals affect blood sugar: type 1 or type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, gestational diabetes, caregivers, or simple curiosity.

Which phones and languages?

Glycéo is coming soon to Android via Google Play. The app is available in French, Arabic, English and Spanish.
