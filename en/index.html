What Glycéo does

Scan

The barcode is enough. Glycéo finds the product thanks to Open Food Facts, the open food database.

Understand

Estimated glycemic index and glycemic load calculated for the portion you actually eat.

Choose better

Alternatives from the same aisle, and simple tips to enjoy your food without guilt.

Your data stays with you

No account, no Glycéo server, no ads, no trackers. Everything is calculated on your phone. The only connection is used to look up a product in Open Food Facts.
