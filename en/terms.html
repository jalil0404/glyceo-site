Important: Glyami is not a medical device

Glyami provides nutrition information. The app does not diagnose, does not calculate any dose and does not replace the advice of your doctor or care team. The glycemic index shown is an estimate: your actual response may differ. Never change your treatment based on the app.

If you feel unwell, or your blood glucose is very low or very high, call emergency services (112 in Europe).

Purpose

Glyami is a free app that helps you understand how food affects blood glucose and choose products more wisely: estimated glycemic index, glycemic load for a portion, a reading of the label (Nutri-Score, Nova, additives), alternatives and tips, shopping lists, meal ideas, loyalty cards and, if you wish, a personal log. Glyami was called Glycéo until October 2026. By using the app, you accept these terms.

Estimates, not certainties

The glycemic index is estimated from published tables and the product's composition. The glycemic load is calculated for the portion you enter. These values are for information only: they vary with preparation, the meal and each person.

Product data

Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database. It may be incomplete or inaccurate. Always check the label, especially if you have an allergy. Data © Open Food Facts contributors, under the ODbL licence; photos under the CC BY-SA licence.

The Nutri-Score, the Nova group and the list of additives are taken from Open Food Facts, as they are calculated there. Nutri-Score is a registered trademark of Santé publique France. The “to watch” note on an additive relies on the assessments taken from Open Food Facts.

Meal ideas and other sources

Meal ideas are cooking suggestions, not a diet or a prescription. Their load per serving is estimated from average carbohydrate values in the Ciqual 2025 food composition table by Anses (published on 19 November 2025), reused under the Licence Ouverte 2.0. Anses has neither reviewed nor endorsed Glyami. Glycemic indexes are estimated from published tables (Atkinson et al., 2008 and 2021; University of Sydney database).

Loyalty cards

Glyami keeps the code of your loyalty cards on your phone to show it at the checkout. The code is redrawn from what you scanned or typed, and some codes are shown as a number only: a shop's reader may sometimes fail to read it, so keep your card within reach. The brands and shops whose names you give to your cards belong to their owners; Glyami has no link with them. Do not store bank cards or identity documents.

Shared lists

When you send a list to someone, by message or with a code to scan, the items you still have to buy leave your phone by the means you choose. The other person makes a new list of them or adds them to their own; the two lists then live separately.
