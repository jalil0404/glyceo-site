Important: Glycéo is not a medical device

Glycéo provides nutrition information. The app does not diagnose, does not calculate any dose and does not replace the advice of your doctor or care team. The glycemic index shown is an estimate: your actual response may differ. Never change your treatment based on the app.

If you feel unwell, or your blood glucose is very low or very high, call emergency services (112 in Europe).

Purpose

Glycéo is a free app that helps you understand how food affects blood glucose: estimated glycemic index, glycemic load for a portion, alternatives and tips, and, if you wish, a personal log. By using the app, you accept these terms.

Estimates, not certainties

The glycemic index is estimated from published tables and the product's composition. The glycemic load is calculated for the portion you enter. These values are for information only: they vary with preparation, the meal and each person.
